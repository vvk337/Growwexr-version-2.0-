## 6. Implementation Guidelines for Content Production

1. **Length & Depth**: Every pillar article must target **1,400 to 1,800 words** with structured H2 and H3 hierarchies, clear definitions, practical examples, and summary takeaways.
2. **YMYL Compliance**: Every article must conclude with a standardized financial risk disclosure stating that research and advisory guidance do not constitute individualized fiduciary advice.
3. **Internal Linking Standard**: Each article must contain **3 to 5 contextual in-text internal links**:
   - At least 1 link to another educational insight (establishing topical clusters).
   - At least 1 link to a relevant commercial service tier (driving conversion).
   - At least 1 link to the consultation booking page (`/contact`).
4. **No Synthesized Dates**: Ensure publication dates are genuinely recorded when articles go live rather than backdated artificially.
