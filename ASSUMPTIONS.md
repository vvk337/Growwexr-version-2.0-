# Project Assumptions & Editorial Disclosures

As instructed in the Website Build Dossier (Master Prompt, Page 5–6), this site operates on strict operational honesty without manufacturing fake testimonials, returns, or unverified claims.

### Documented Assumptions

1. **Brand Identity**:
   - Primary display name confirmed from vector asset: **Growwezy Global Tech** (Short name: **Growwezy**).
   - Brand color accents (purple and green gradients inside the logo) are respected, while the primary site palette adheres strictly to the Sterling-inspired modern white, pale ice, and deep navy visual language.

2. **Target Audience & Localization**:
   - Target clients: NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) and International clients based in the Middle East (UAE, GCC).
   - Initial launch is in English. Layouts are constructed with fluid containers ready for future multi-language/RTL support without refactoring DOM order.

3. **Service Offerings**:
   - Confirmed primary service routes:
     1. `Forex Trading Advisory`
     2. `Financial Asset Management`
   - Additional topics (Market Research, Strategic Consultation) are categorized as aspects of the primary services, not separate unverified commercial entities.

4. **Honest Proof & Social Evidence**:
   - Case studies, testimonials, and client logos from the reference template are strictly excluded until confirmed data is provided.
   - Corresponding UI modules are built and ready behind feature flags in `src/config/site.ts`.

5. **Form & External Endpoints**:
   - Until a production webhook/form API is connected, the contact form operates in an honest "Preview Mode" with clear user messaging, rather than pretending to transmit data.
