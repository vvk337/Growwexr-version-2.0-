## 4. Keyword Cannibalization Analysis

Keyword cannibalization occurs when multiple pages on the same website compete for identical search intents, splitting organic ranking power.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   CANNIBALIZATION AUDIT FINDINGS                       │
├──────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┬────────────────┤
│ Potential Conflict Area      │ Risk Level             │ Strategic Fix  │
├──────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┼────────────────┤
│ Forex Signals vs. Combo      │ High                   │ Differentiate Combo around cross-asset hedging and diversification. │
│ Comex Signals vs. Combo      │ High                   │ Keep Comex strictly focused on pure bullion (XAU/XAG). │
│ Services Index vs. Homepage  │ Moderate               │ Target brand + broad firm mandate on Home; target commercial plan comparison on Services. │
│ About Us vs. Our Approach    │ Low–Moderate           │ About targets corporate origin & longevity; Approach targets client workflow. │
└──────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┴────────────────┘
```

### Specific Recommendations to Eliminate Overlap:
1. **Differentiate `/services/combo-signals`**:
   - *Problem*: Currently, the Combo page reads like a simple concatenation of the Forex and Comex pages.
   - *Fix*: Re-target the Combo page specifically toward **"Cross-Asset Portfolio Management"** and **"Hedging Currency Volatility with Precious Metals"**. This creates a distinct search intent that does not compete directly with single-asset searchers.
2. **Clarify Homepage vs. Services Index**:
   - Homepage title focuses on the institutional firm mandate: *"Growwezy Global Tech | Forex Advisory & Asset Management"*.
   - Services Index focuses strictly on plan selection: *"Trading Signals & Account Management Services | Growwezy Global Tech"*.
